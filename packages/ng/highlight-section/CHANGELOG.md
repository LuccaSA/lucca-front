### 22.1.1

#### Deprecated

- `lu-highlight-section` component and `.highlightSection` class, to be replaced by `lu-box` with extra options in 22.2.

### 22.0.0

#### Added

- New `lu-highlight-section` component: a visual container highlighting a key information or a priority action, with a free content slot, three themes (`white`, `light`, `dark`), optional bubble ornaments and an optional illustration.
